- **3D Solid Geometry & Viewport Rendering Standards (Walls, Slabs, Columns, Beams, Openings)**:
  1. **Monolithic Surfaces (No Grid Seams)**: Slabs and walls must be rendered as monolithic single-polygon faces (`render_cap_face`, `emit_pier_span`). NEVER subdivide horizontal or vertical surfaces into grid tiles, as `egui::Shape::convex_polygon` sub-pixel anti-aliasing produces visible light-gray seam lines in Shaded, Consistent Colors, and Hidden Line modes.
  2. **Watertight Caps & Bottom Occlusion**: Foundation slabs must generate solid bottom caps and perimeter rim faces (`occlude_from_below`) so walls and foundations never appear hollow or transparent when orbiting underneath. Roof slabs strictly occlude interior walls from exterior viewpoints.
  3. **Solid Columns & Beams**: Columns and beams must be rendered as opaque 6-sided solid prisms (`render_column_solid`, `render_beam_solid`) with camera normal culling and directional shading, never as hollow or transparent shells.
  4. **Door & Window Opening Cutouts & Vertical Jamb Lines**:
     - Wall piers must terminate strictly at opening bounds `[d0, d1]`. NEVER expand full-height pier quads into the opening hole (`c.z_bot..c.z_top`). Coplanar anti-aliasing seam expansion (`±8mm`) is strictly restricted to sill (`z0..c.z_bot`) and lintel (`c.z_top..z1`) quads outside the opening hole.
     - Enforce the 5-layer depth ordering: (1) Recessed panel, (2) Jamb/sill/soffit reveals, (3) Adjacent piers (`closer_pier_depth`), (4) Coplanar sill & lintel fills, (5) Crisp outer opening cutout frame (`[sill, right_jamb, lintel_head, left_jamb]`), guaranteeing left and right vertical jamb lines are 100% visible on both exterior and interior faces.
  5. **Mitered Wall Corners & T-Joints**: Wall corner intersections must use mitered footprints (`Wall::mitered_footprint`) and cross-joint cutout spans (`w.cross_joint_spans`) to avoid overlapping geometry or missing corner faces.
  6. **Pitched & Flat Roof Shapes**: `Roof` supports 4 architectural shapes (`RoofShape::Hip`, `RoofShape::Gable`, `RoofShape::Shed`, `RoofShape::Flat`) via `r.faces(level_z)` and `render_roof_solid` with watertight sloped planes, soffits, and eave fascias.
  7. **IFC4 Property Sets & Psets**: `Wall`, `Door`, `Window`, `Ceiling`, `Floor/Slab`, `Roof`, `Column`, and `Beam` maintain standard IFC4 Psets (`Pset_*Common`) and `IFCRELDEFINESBYPROPERTIES` in `writer.rs` and editable inspector panels in `properties.rs`.
  8. **Process Locking**: When compiling/linking on Windows, terminate running instances (`taskkill /f /im arch3d.exe >nul 2>&1`) to avoid `x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc` linker locks. In batch scripts, always escape `&` as `^&`.

  11. **Native SketchUp (.skp) Model Import Standard**:
     - Uses clean-room `openskp` crate (zero proprietary Trimble SDK dependencies) to parse both modern VFF ZIP containers (SketchUp 2021+) and legacy MFC CArchive streams (SketchUp 2013-2020).
     - Coordinates scale strictly from OpenSKP meters to Arch3D millimeters by $\times 1000.0$ ($1\text{ m} = 1000\text{ mm}$), preserving Right-Handed $+Z$ Up coordinate system without destructive axis swaps.
     - Scene graph traversal (`Model::scene()`) concatenates 4×4 transformation matrices across nested component/group definitions into world space.
     - Prunes subtrees where `node.hidden` is true (adhering to OpenSKP WYSIWYG semantics), completely preventing hidden geometry runs from leaking into world space.
     - Eliminates exploding geometry rays and starbursts:
       1. Non-planar quads are decomposed into two triangles along the shortest diagonal (`min(d02, d13)`), with internal diagonal chord edges flagged as soft (`hard = false`).
       2. N-gons are projected into an exact orthonormal tangent frame `(U, V)` perpendicular to the Newell normal before ear-clipping triangulation, preserving orientation and avoiding foreshortening/distortion.
       3. Edge soft/smooth/hidden status is verified against `mesh.edges`; internal diagonals and soft/smoothed curved contours are flagged as `hard = false`, guaranteeing smooth shading without wireframe spiderwebs.
       4. Consecutive duplicate vertices within 0.1 mm tolerance are deduplicated, and vertex indices are validated against mesh boundaries.

       1. Group and component definition hierarchies are tracked (`solid.group_name` and `solid.is_group`) and displayed in the Properties inspector.
       2. Materials extract texture metadata, including filename (`texture_name`) and embedded binary image bytes (`texture_bytes`, PNG/JPEG).
     - Converts imported geometry into `Element::FreeformSolid` with `PrimitiveKind::Mesh { faces }`, immediately rendering across 3D, floor plan, elevation, and section viewports with full visual styles.

   29. **True 3D Ray-Polygon Intersection Picking Engine (`canvas.rs`)**:
      - **Screen-to-World Ray Unrotation**: Clicking anywhere in the 3D viewport unrotates screen coordinates $(v_x, v_z)$ into an exact world-space camera ray $(\text{ray\_origin}, \text{ray\_dir})$ matching the active view's pitch, yaw, pan, and zoom for both Orthographic and Perspective modes.
      - **Pass 1 (Direct Surface Hits)**: Back-facing faces ($\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{d} \ge -10^{-5}$) are culled. Solves exact ray-plane intersection $t = \mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{p}_0 - \mathbf{o}) / (\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{d})$ for $t > 0$. Projects hit point $\mathbf{p}_{\text{hit}}$ onto dominant normal 2D plane and validates point-in-polygon with 25mm boundary tolerance. The candidate with the minimum physical ray distance $t$ along the ray wins ($t_{\text{eff}} = t + \text{bias}$). Surfaces closer to the camera ALWAYS have smaller positive $t$, eliminating coordinate inversion or depth sign bugs at any 360° orbit angle.
      - **Pass 2 (Depth-Weighted Near-Edge Fallback)**: Runs only when Pass 1 finds no surface hit (empty space within 8px of an edge). Evaluates 3D face depth $t_{\text{face}}$ and 2D pixel distance $d \le 8\text{px}$ using depth-weighted scoring ($\text{score} = t_{\text{face}} + d \times 50.0$), guaranteeing foreground wall and beam edges always defeat distant background floor slabs or interior walls.
